# Key sources (check current official text)

- **EU/UK privacy:** GDPR (Reg. 2016/679, eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj); UK GDPR, DPA 2018, Data (Use and Access) Act 2025 (ico.org.uk); ePrivacy Directive 2002/58/EC; PECR 2003; EDPB Guidelines 05/2020 on consent; CNIL cookies guidance; EDPB Opinion 28/2024 on AI models; SCCs Decision (EU) 2021/914; Planet49 (C-673/17).
- **US privacy:** CCPA/CPRA and regulations (cppa.ca.gov/regulations); CalOPPA; Global Privacy Control (globalprivacycontrol.org) and CCPA Regs §7025; COPPA Rule 16 CFR Part 312; Washington My Health My Data Act; FTC GoodRx action (2023); FTC blog on quietly changing terms for AI training (Feb 2024).
- **Marketing:** CAN-SPAM (ftc.gov compliance guide); TCPA; CASL.
- **Consumer protection:** EU UCPD 2005/29/EC as amended; Consumer Rights Directive 2011/83/EU and 2023/2673; UK Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013; UK DMCCA 2024; FTC Act §5 and Advertising Substantiation policy; FTC Dark Patterns report (2022); FTC fees rule 16 CFR 464; Cal. Civ. Code §1770(a)(29); ROSCA; California Automatic Renewal Law (AB 2863); Custom Communications v. FTC (8th Cir. 2025); FTC reviews rule 16 CFR 465; Endorsement Guides 16 CFR 255; Lanham Act §43(a).
- **Contracts and disclosures:** Berman v. Freedom Financial Network (9th Cir. 2022); E-SIGN Act; e-Commerce Directive Art 5; Digital Services Act; German DDG §5 (Impressum).
- **Accessibility:** WCAG 2.2 (w3.org/TR/WCAG22); European Accessibility Act 2019/882 and EN 301 549; DOJ ADA web guidance (ada.gov/resources/web-guidance).
- **AI:** EU AI Act (Reg. 2024/1689) Art 50 and Commission FAQ.
- **IP:** OSI, SPDX, GNU, Apache licence resources; US Copyright Office AI guidance (copyright.gov/ai); DMCA 17 U.S.C. §512, 37 CFR §201.38, DMCA Directory (copyright.gov/dmca-directory).
- **Platforms:** Apple App Review Guidelines (5.1.1, 3.1.2) and privacy manifests; Google Play Developer Policy (User Data, account deletion, Data safety, subscriptions).
- **Security claims:** AICPA SOC suite / Trust Services Criteria.

If web search is available and a finding turns on a date, threshold or rule that may have changed since October 2026, look it up before stating it as current.
