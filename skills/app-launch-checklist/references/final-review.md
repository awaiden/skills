# Final review before launch

No checklist makes a product mistake-free. What it can do is make sure the right questions were asked, real behaviour was tested, and qualified people were brought in where the answer depends on circumstances.

- **Review:** lawyer review of privacy policy, terms, refund and subscription flows and marketing claims per launch market; accessibility specialist or disabled users test key flows with assistive tech, not just automated tools; confirm which laws apply based on user location, data processed and role in each data flow.
- **Re-test:** repeat Verify steps on the **final release build**, not a dev version; public documents, store listings and the live product describe the same behaviour.
- **Keep it current:** assign an owner and next review date per section; re-run relevant sections on every new feature, vendor, data category, pricing model or market.
